## About Me
Hi, I'm Thomas Wang (Xiye). 
My research areas in audio include but are not limited to:

- **Technical Sound / Audio Design**: Solving bugs in audio middleware and game engine integration. Developing internal tools to provide the team with a complete set of efficient automated audio workflows
- **Audio Programming**: Researching the implementation of common audio effect algorithms, and implementing them in Wwise or JUCE
- **Sound Design**: In addition to interactive sound design using sound libraries, I'm particularly interested in sound design from scratch through Foley and synthesizers
- **Sound Engineering (Recording, Acoustic Design)**: Experience in orchestral recording, instrument recording, vocal recording, and independently designed small commercial recording studios with technical support
- **Analog Synthesizer**: Beginner enthusiast of analog synthesizers and Eurorack
